About

I am a incoming Robotics Ph.D. student at Georgia Tech, and I'm excited to join Professor Hiro Ono in the Planetary Robotics Lab on developing 3D perception systems for planetary robots!

Previously, I earned my Master's degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Robotics track) from the University of Michigan, where I worked with Professor Ram Vasudevan in the ROAHM Lab on physically grounded 3D scene reconstruction and interactive exploration.

Before that, I earned dual Bachelor's degrees from National Taiwan University, double majoring in Mechanical Engineering and Physics. My undergraduate research experience includes intelligent elderly-care robotics, adviced by Professor Li-Chen Fu at Advanced Control Laboratory, and computational astrophysics with Dr. Hsien Shang at Academia Sinica, Institute of Astonomy and Astrophysics (ASIAA).
