About

I am a Masters student in Electrical and Computer Engineering (Robotics track) at the University of Michigan, where I work with Prof. Ram Vasudevan in the ROAHM Lab on physical accurate 3D scene reconstruction.

My research interests include robust robot perception and planning, and human-level perception and understanding for robots. Specifically, my current research focuses on combining robot vision and interactions to autonomously construct 3D representations enriched with both visual and physical properties, enabling robust real-world robot operation with such representations.
